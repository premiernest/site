Before you book

Good to know.

How can I contact PremierNest?+

Whether you're looking to stay for business or leisure, or just have a question about the flat, our team is on hand: call +44 3330 508 780 (24 hours), WhatsApp +44 7832 647103, or email admin@premiernest.org.

What time is check-in and check-out?+

Check-in is from 3:00pm and check-out is by 10:00am. Early check-in or late check-out can often be arranged — just ask before your stay, or via the check-in guide sent closer to arrival.

Why book direct instead of through Airbnb or Booking.com?+

Booking direct means no platform mark-up, the most flexible cancellation terms we offer, and direct contact with us if your plans change — the same apartment, the fairer rate.

Do I need to sign anything before I stay?+

Yes — every guest completes a short digital guest agreement (house rules and terms & conditions) as part of online check-in, along with everything you need to know for arrival: door codes, wifi, and local tips.

Is a security deposit required?+

No deposit is taken at booking. Guests remain liable for any damage caused during their stay, as set out in the guest agreement.

Is the apartment insured and compliant?+

Yes. The apartment is covered by full insurance and operated in line with current UK short-let safety and regulatory requirements.

Can I view the apartment before booking?+

You're welcome to visit the flat in person before booking so you can see it meets what you need — just email us to arrange a time. For monthly or longer stays we can also schedule a live video walkthrough if an in-person viewing isn't practical.

What is a serviced apartment?+

A serviced apartment is fully furnished accommodation available for temporary rent, suited to short stays, medium stays or long-term stays, with hotel-like extras laid on during your stay such as cleaning, maintenance and responsive support. Apartments are normally self-contained, with a fully fitted kitchen and a private bathroom, plus separate living and sleeping areas depending on the size and configuration.
